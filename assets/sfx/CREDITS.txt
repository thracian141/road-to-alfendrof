Sound effects in this folder were cut and converted from these free packs:

- Deadly Kombat Free Library (hits, blocks, whooshes, gore, blades) - Daniel Sounds Good, https://danielsoundsgood.itch.io/free-deadly-kombat-sound-effects
- Magic Spell SFX Soundpack (fire, dark, holy, earth, wind, water, lightning) - Leonardo Calvo / Soundrack, CC BY 4.0, https://thesoundrack.itch.io/freemagicspellsfx
- Gun sounds (gunshots) - alienanthony, public domain, https://alienanthony.itch.io/gun-sounds
- Owlish Media Sound Effects (footsteps, cloth, paper, UI, scifi) - Owlish Media, CC0, https://opengameart.org/content/sound-effects-pack
- Foley pack (glass, leather) - https://opengameart.org/content/super-foley-pack
- Whip sound - Reemax, CC BY, https://opengameart.org/content/whip-sound
- Free Audio Creature Sample Pack (goblin squeaks) - fairlight1337, https://fairlight1337.itch.io/free-audio-creature-sample-pack
